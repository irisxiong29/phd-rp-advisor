# RP Review Rubric

Use this for Stage 5 and Stage 6.

## Priority definitions

### Major
Affects the viability of the proposal:
- unclear or weak research problem
- unsupported research gap
- theory does not answer the question
- methodology cannot generate required evidence
- unmanageable scope
- major citation inaccuracy
- internal contradiction

### Moderate
Does not collapse the proposal but weakens persuasiveness:
- subsection lacks a clear argument
- literature is descriptive
- transitions obscure logic
- case selection under-justified
- RQ wording is imprecise
- contribution is generic
- supervisor fit is under-signalled

### Minor
Local presentation issue:
- grammar
- collocation
- repetition
- punctuation
- citation-format inconsistency
- unnecessary wording

## Diagnostic questions

### Title
- Does it identify the central phenomenon/problem?
- Is it too broad or too case-specific?
- Does disciplinary positioning match the target?

### Abstract
- Does it summarise the whole design?
- Does it include problem/question, object/data, method, and intended contribution?
- Is it merely a compressed copy of the Introduction?

### Introduction
- Does it establish why the phenomenon matters?
- Does it move toward a research problem rather than only provide background?
- Does it avoid reviewing the whole literature too early?

### Literature Review / Theoretical Frames
- Does each subsection make an argument?
- Is there progression between subsections?
- Are theoretical concepts defined and operationally useful?
- Are recent and foundational works balanced?
- Does the review culminate in the project's position?

### Research Gap and RQs
- Is the gap demonstrated rather than asserted?
- Does the main RQ directly answer the gap?
- Do secondary questions divide the analytical work?
- Are concepts in the RQs actually researchable?

### Methodology
- Why these cases/participants/platforms/texts?
- What data will each method produce?
- How will data be analysed?
- Is access realistic?
- Are ethical issues recognised where needed?
- Does every major RQ have an evidence pathway?

### Contribution
Distinguish:
- empirical contribution
- conceptual/theoretical contribution
- methodological contribution
- practical contribution

Do not claim all four by default.

## Section-by-section comment format

For each important issue give:
- Location
- Priority
- Problem
- Why it matters
- Recommended revision
- Optional example only when useful

## Final review rule

At Stage 6, do not reopen settled conceptual decisions unless they create a clear error.
The final pass should be conservative.
