# Output Templates

These are defaults. Adapt when the user gives a specific format.

## Stage 1 — Icebreaker lesson

### Slide 1 — Student Research Profile
- education / discipline
- relevant projects
- practical or field experience

### Slide 2 — Emerging Research Interests
Use 3–5 clusters with evidence.

### Slide 3 — Existing Theoretical Familiarity
For each: theory/concept | evidence from materials | confidence | possible future relevance

### Slide 4 — Existing Methodological Familiarity
method | prior use | apparent strength | what to clarify

### Slide 5 — Provisional Research Spaces
2–4 broad areas, not final RP topics.

### Slide 6 — Supervisor / Programme Fit
supervisor theme | overlap | possible mismatch | question to clarify

### Slide 7 — Questions for the Icebreaker
Prioritised questions that cannot be answered from files.

### Slide 8 — Next-Step Evidence
What the adviser should collect after class.

## Stage 2 — Topic brainstorming

Start with a short comparative table:
Topic | Core problem | Main theoretical conversation | Main method | Best fit | Key risk

Then for each of 3–4 topics:

### Topic X — [Working Title]
**Core research problem:**  
**Why it fits this student:**  
**Main RQ:**  
**Expansion paths / sub-question logic:**  
**Possible theoretical architecture:**  
**Methods and data:**  
**Core benchmark literature:**  
**Advantages:**  
**Risks:**  
**Adaptability:**  

End with:
- strongest option(s)
- what still needs verification before final selection
Do not force a single winner if evidence remains genuinely mixed.

## Stage 3 — 800-word outline

# Main Title
[Title]

**Other title choices**
- [discipline/supervisor adaptation 1]
- [adaptation 2]
- [adaptation 3 if useful]

## Abstract
Function: compressed whole-project overview.
Include:
- phenomenon/context
- research problem
- main question/aim
- empirical focus
- method
- expected conceptual contribution

Do not mirror the Introduction sentence by sentence.

**中文提示：** one short paragraph explaining what the student should later expand.

## Introduction
Function: lead from context to the research problem.
Typical movement:
context → specific change/tension → why existing explanations are insufficient → project focus.

Personal academic background may appear in 1–2 sentences only if it establishes preparation/access.

**中文提示：** explain expansion logic.

## Literature Review and Theoretical Frames

### 1. [Conversation/strand]
- key argument
- key references
- what this establishes

### 2. [Conversation/strand]
...

### 3. [Conversation/strand, if needed]
...

End the section by clarifying how these conversations produce the research problem.

**中文提示：** explain how to turn bullets into literature dialogue rather than summaries.

## Research Gap and Research Questions
**Research gap:** [precise gap]

**Main RQ:** [one main question]

**Secondary-question logic:**
- SQ1 should examine...
- SQ2 should examine...
- SQ3 should examine... (only if needed)

Do not write final sub-questions if the user wants room for student development.

**中文提示：** explain derivation from gap.

## Methodology
For each method:
- purpose
- evidence/data
- relation to RQ

Include case selection / participant logic where relevant.
Mention ethics only when materially relevant.

**中文提示：** explain what needs operational detail in the full RP.

## Expected Contribution
Keep brief and proportionate.
Prefer 1–2 defensible contributions.

**中文提示：** explain what evidence is needed to justify the contribution.

## References
APA unless user specifies another style.
Add verified DOI links where they exist.

## Stage 4 — Chinese annotated outline

Retain the outline's section order.

For each section use:
### [Section]
**这一部分要完成什么：**  
**建议扩写顺序：**  
**需要补充的文献/材料：**  
**与上一部分/下一部分的关系：**  
**学生写作时特别注意：**  
**建议字数：**  

Do not simply translate the English outline.

## Stage 5 — Draft review

# Overall Assessment
2–4 paragraphs.

# Major Issues
Table:
Location | Problem | Why it matters | Revision direction

# Moderate Issues
Same format.

# Section-by-section Notes

# Literature / Reference Verification
List only references that require correction/checking plus important missing literature.

# Recommended Revision Order
Numbered sequence from conceptual repairs to language polishing.

---

## Stage 6 — Final proofreading

Default to one of two modes depending on user request:

### Mode A — Comment-only
Location | Original issue | Minimal correction | Reason

### Mode B — Clean edited version + change log
Return:
1. clean revised text
2. concise list of substantive wording changes

Do not generate a large commentary report when the user only wants final language polishing.
