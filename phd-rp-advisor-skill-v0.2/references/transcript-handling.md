# Handling Unlabelled Class Transcripts

Class transcripts may merge adviser and student speech.

## Do not over-attribute

Never write:
"The student definitely believes X"
based on one isolated sentence when speaker identity is unclear.

Use confidence levels internally:
- High confidence: repeated first-person experience, autobiographical detail, explicit agreement/decision
- Medium confidence: idea appears repeatedly and is adopted in later discussion
- Low confidence: isolated proposal/question with no later confirmation

## Reconstruct decisions, not speakers

Extract four layers:

### 1. Student evidence
Likely autobiographical or competence-related material:
- prior projects
- skills
- experiences
- access to communities/cases
- preferences and dislikes
- career plans

### 2. Proposed ideas
All candidate topics, concepts, cases, methods, authors.

### 3. Reactions
Evidence that an idea was:
- accepted
- rejected
- questioned
- postponed
- compared with another idea

### 4. End-state
By the end of the transcript:
- what direction appears preferred
- what was explicitly ruled out
- what needs further checking
- what evidence was requested for next time

## Output language

When attribution is uncertain, use:
- "The discussion suggests..."
- "A possible direction raised in the session was..."
- "The transcript appears to converge on..."
- "This point should be confirmed rather than treated as settled."

Do not clutter the final deliverable with confidence labels unless uncertainty materially affects the recommendation.
